In het kort

Wanneer is Elfia Arcen 2026?

Zaterdag 19 en zondag 20 september 2026. Zaterdag van 10:00 tot 22:00, zondag van 10:00 tot 19:00.

Waar is het?

Kasteeltuinen Arcen, Lingsforterweg 26, 5944 BE Arcen.

Mag ik tussendoor het terrein verlaten?

Ja. Voor 2026 geldt onbeperkt in- en uitlopen: je mag zo vaak als je wilt naar buiten en weer naar binnen, via de officiële uitgangen.

Wat kost het voor kinderen?

Kinderen van 0 t/m 2 jaar mogen gratis naar binnen. Little Elf-tickets gelden voor 3 t/m 11 jaar.

Mag mijn hond mee?

Ja, aangelijnd. Ook paarden, vogels en fretten (aangelijnd) zijn welkom. Reptielen, hagedissen, konijnen en andere exotische huisdieren niet. In Kasteel Arcen zelf zijn honden niet toegestaan.

Mag ik mijn metalen zwaard meenemen?

Nee. Sinds november 2025 zijn metalen wapens in geen enkele vorm toegestaan, ook niet als ze decoratief, volledig bot of vast aan je kostuum zitten. Alleen zachte, niet-metalen props zijn toegestaan.

Mag ik eigen eten en drinken meenemen?

In principe niet. Wel toegestaan: één ongeopende plastic waterfles van maximaal 500 ml, babyvoeding, en eten of drinken voor medische of dieetredenen (mogelijk met medisch bewijs). Glas is overal verboden.

Waar mag ik roken?

De Kasteeltuinen Arcen zijn rookvrij. Roken en vapen mag alleen in de aangewezen rookzones, die op de plattegrond staan. Cannabis is overal verboden.

Mag ik met een drone vliegen?

Nee. Drones zijn voor iedereen verboden, ook voor fotografen en pers. Vliegen boven evenementen en mensenmenigten is bij wet verboden.

Is het terrein toegankelijk met een rolstoel?

Grotendeels wel. Er is rolstoelverhuur bij de entree en een lift in Kasteel Arcen. De rotstuin en midgetgolf is niet rolstoeltoegankelijk. Er is apart parkeren met een officieel erkende gehandicaptenparkeerkaart.

Kan ik met het paard komen?

Ja, maar er kunnen maar vijf ruiters per dag hun paard parkeren. Een paardenparkeerticket kost €1, inclusief hooi en vers water.

Wordt mijn tas gecontroleerd?

Dat kan. De beveiliging voert bij de entree tascontroles uit, altijd beleefd en respectvol.

Let op: regels kunnen per editie verschillen. Deze antwoorden gelden voor Arcen; raadpleeg altijd de huisregels van de locatie die je bezoekt.

Algemene informatie

What is the 'Kingdom of Elfia' festival?

The Kingdom of Elfia is a magical community festival born in 2001, where imagination knows no borders. It has grown into Europe's most enchanting gathering for those who love to express themselves through cosplay, fantasy, historical, or other creative subcultures. In Elfia, everyone is free to become whoever they dream to be, united by a shared respect for themselves and for others. It's not just a festival, but a living kingdom where fantasy and reality dance together, inviting all to move the magic.

What is the story behind the Kingdom of Elfia?

The Kingdom of Elfia festival is more than an event. It's a living portal. Each festival forms one end of a wormhole that connects our world to Planet Eve, a distant utopia in another galaxy. On Eve lies the continent of Elfia, home to humans, dragons, elves, demons, dwarves, and hybrid beings like elmans, draeries, and degons. Its vast history unfolds on http://kingdomofelfia.com/drach-oc, while the living story continues through the manga Drach-oc on http://kingdomofelfia.com/manga

We will then refund the incorrectly purchased ticket (excluding service fees). Please note: the refund can only be processed once the correct ticket has been purchased.

If the correct day is sold out and you are no longer able to attend, you may resell your ticket safely through our official resale platform, TicketSwap.

Props en wapens

What kinds of prop weapons are allowed?

Only prop weapons made of soft, NON-METAL materials such as plastic, rubber, foam, or other flexible materials are allowed. These items must be clearly recognisable as props and must not resemble real or functional weapons.

All props must be completely blunt, with no sharp edges, pointed tips, or hard impact surfaces. Even if the prop weapon is attached to your outfit and cannot be drawn, it must still meet this requirement.

What about wooden prop weapons?

Wooden prop weapons are allowed only if they are lightweight, completely blunt, and generally under about 1 metre in length. Simple wooden staffs within this range are usually acceptable. Larger or heavier items (such as mallets or large hammers), or anything over 1 metre that could cause real impact, may be refused at security's discretion. Attendees may bring such items at their own risk, but approval cannot be guaranteed.

Are 3D-printed prop weapons or guns allowed at the festival grounds?

Only 3D-printed props made entirely of plastic, rubber, or other soft materials are allowed. 3D-printed items made of metal or other hard materials are strictly prohibited. Additionally, 3D-printed or replica firearms are not allowed if they resemble real guns in size, shape, or colour, even if non-functional. Under Dutch law, such items are still considered real weapons and cannot be brought into public spaces or events.

Can I bring a metal weapon if it's just for display or part of my costume?

No. For safety reasons, metal weapons of any kind are not allowed, even if they are decorative, completely blunt, or attached to your costume and cannot be drawn.

Will my prop weapon be checked?

Yes. All props weapons will be inspected by the security team at the entrance. Final approval is always given by the security staff on the day of the festival.

What happens if my prop weapon is not approved?

If your item is deemed unsafe to be permitted inside, you will be asked to return it to your vehicle or hand it over to security to collect when you leave the festival.

Can I get special permission to bring a real or metal weapon if I promise not to use it?

No. Exceptions are not possible. Real or metal weapons are no longer permitted at Elfia under any circumstances, even if you promise not to use them. The same safety policy applies to ALL visitors, participants, and exhibitors alike to maintain a safe and welcoming environment for everyone.

Why has the weapon policy changed?

In light of recent developments and changing public safety regulations, Elfia has reviewed its weapon policy in consultation with local authorities and its organising board. As one of the last festivals that still allowed metal prop weapons, we have decided that it is no longer acceptable or responsible to do so. This decision follows new social circumstances and increased awareness of public safety.

Therefore, from now on, NO METAL WEAPONS of any kind are permitted within the festival grounds.

Algemene vragen

Are the dates for upcoming Elfia editions already known?

Yes, we have already scheduled several dates. In 2026, Vorden: 8, 9, and 10 May. For Arcen: 19 and 20 September.

Is there an ATM on Elfia?

For Elfia Arcen 2026, there will not be an ATM on-site.

All Elfia beverage bars operate cashlessly, and most food vendors and caterers also accept cashless payments. However, some merchants may still accept cash, so it's a good idea to bring some with you.

If you need to withdraw cash during your visit, unlimited re-entry is allowed, and there is an ATM approximately 400 metres from the festival entrance in Arcen village. We recommend bringing enough cash or a payment card to ensure you're prepared for all purchases.

Do you have to pay to use the toilet?

At Elfia, the toilets have been free since 2023 and have also become gender-neutral. This means that there is no longer a distinction between ladies' and men's toilets. As a result, the toilets are accessible to everyone. Despite the visitor limit, the total number of toilets has been significantly increased to meet the demand. Our toilets are regularly cleaned by a dedicated team responsible for this task.

Can I fly a drone above Elfia?

Unfortunately, it is not legally permitted to fly a drone above Elfia as a private individual due to the presence of crowds and buildings. For more information, visit Rijksoverheid.nl. Therefore, for safety and security reasons, the use of drones is strictly prohibited at Elfia, whether by photographers, media, or visitors.

Can I breathe fire on the terrain during Elfia?

Can you breathe fire? Awesome! For the safety of all attendees and the preservation of the site, we kindly ask you not to carry out fire-breathing actions during the event without permission. If you have a special talent for fire breathing and would like to show it off, please contact us by filling out the form on the entertainment page. This way we can discuss the possibilities to integrate your talent in a safe and controlled implementation.

Can I bring my own food and drinks?

You are not allowed to bring your own food and drinks onto the site. About a few sandwiches, one or two snacks and a bottle of water (500ml), we do not bother. However, large quantities of food will be rejected by Security. There is no intention of bringing whole picnic baskets of course-dinners or second and third breakfasts. (Sorry, hobbits!) You are also not allowed to bring alcohol onto the grounds yourself, but it can be ordered from the various bars. Luckily, there are also a lot of different caterers on site! For vegetarians, vegans or people who want to eat gluten-free: check our catering overview.

Can I leave the festival grounds for a while and come back later on the same day with the same ticket?

Yes! For the 2026 edition, unlimited re-entry is now allowed. You can leave and return to the festival grounds as many times as you like throughout the day. Just make sure to exit via the official exit points. This policy gives you full freedom to explore, take breaks, or meet friends, so you can enjoy Elfia on your own terms!

I have (certain type of disease/medical condition) and there are medical or dietary requirements that I need to follow to get through the day. For this reason, can I bring my own food or drinks?

In general, visitors are not allowed to bring their own food or drinks onto the festival grounds. However, if you have a serious medical condition that requires specific dietary items, you may bring a small amount of food and drinks for personal use. This includes items such as a few sandwiches, snacks, or specific necessities like dextrose tablets, fruit juice, or a can of cola. Please keep it limited to what is necessary to manage your condition, as large bags of meals or beverages are strictly prohibited. Please be aware that to be permitted entry with these items, you must be able to show valid medical proof, such as a doctor's note or report, to the security staff at the entrance. Failure to provide proof may result in your food or drinks not being allowed onto the festival grounds.

Is there an age policy for making tattoos at Elfia?

Yes. The following age restrictions apply:

  • Under 12: Tattoos are not permitted. No exceptions.
  • 12–16: Written parental consent is required, and a parent must be present during the procedure. Tattoos on the head, neck, wrists, or hands are not permitted.
  • 16–17: A person may consent independently, but tattoos on the head, neck, wrists, or hands are not permitted until the age of 18.
  • 18+: No age-related restrictions apply.
Is it possible to store my belongings somewhere?

Yes, it is certainly possible to store your belongings safely during Elfia. We offer lockers that you can find near the entrance. These lockers come in three different sizes: Dwarf (small), Human (medium) and Giant (large). All lockers are equipped with a handy USB connection, so you can charge your mobile devices, for example.

As the lockers are very popular, we recommend booking a locker in advance. This is because they go fast! You reserve a locker by buying a ticket for it, simply in our ticket shop: https://elfia.com/Ticket.html

Before the event, you will receive a code to open your locker with. Make sure you do not leave items in the locker, because the next day the access code will change and you will no longer be able to access your items left behind.

Toegankelijkheid voor mensen met een beperking

Is there parking available for visitors with disabilities?

Yes. There will be a dedicated parking area for visitors with a valid disabled parking permit, located closer to the festival entrance. Please note that the permit must be an officially recognised one, such as the European Disabled Parking Card issued by the Dutch or German government

I use a van or bus with a wheelchair lift. Can I park it at Elfia?

Buses and campers are generally not permitted in the regular parking area, but exceptions can be made for accessibility vehicles. If your van or bus is required for mobility reasons, you may bring it to the festival grounds. However, please note that parking is only allowed for vehicles up to 5 meters in length.

Can visitors with limited mobility access all areas of the festival?

Not all areas of the festival are accessible for visitors with limited mobility or wheelchair users

Fotografen en pers

Can I bring my camera or SLR to Elfia as a photographer?

Yes, you are very welcome to bring your camera or SLR to Elfia for personal use at Elfia. However, please remember that photography and filming are only allowed for private, non-commercial purposes. Any form of commercial recording and photographing are strictly prohibited under our Terms and Conditions.

Do I need a photo pass or press card?

No, you do not need a special photographer pass or press card to bring your camera to Elfia. Simply purchasing a regular festival ticket is sufficient to enjoy and photograph the event.

Can I get a free ticket as a photographer or member of the press?

No, we do not provide free tickets to photographers. All visitors, including photographers, are required to purchase a regular festival ticket. Official press coverage and photography are exclusively managed by Elfia's own press team, and we do not issue external press passes.

What kind of photography equipment is allowed?

You may bring semi or professional photography gear such as lenses, tripods, lighting equipment, and power storage. If you need additional power, please bring your own generator or power bank, as no power outlets are provided on-site.

However, please keep in mind that drones are strictly prohibited within the castle grounds for safety and privacy reasons.

Can photography and video recordings be used during Elfia events, and how are they used?

Yes. Photography and video recording may take place at Elfia events. By attending, visitors agree that they may appear in photos or videos, which Elfia may use for editorial, informational, archival, and promotional purposes related to Elfia, Kingdom of Elfia, Drach-oc, and related events.

This includes use on websites, social media, newsletters, advertising, ticket campaigns, and other event-related materials without needing individual consent.

However, if images are used for separate commercial products such as merchandise, calendars, or posters sold independently, Elfia will request additional permission when necessary.

Parkeren

How do I purchase a parking ticket?

For the 2026 Arcen edition, parking tickets will be sold separately by the castle. You can purchase them here: Kasteeltuinen Parking Ticket Page. For parking-related questions in this location, please email: info@kasteeltuinen.nl

For other editions, parking tickets can be purchased directly via the official Elfia website ticket page. We strongly recommend booking your parking ticket in advance to ensure smooth access on the day of the event.

Is there a vehicle size limit?

Yes. The maximum vehicle length per parking ticket is 5 metres. Exceptions can be granted upon prior approval for special cases. For further information contact elfoffice@elfia.com.

Can I park overnight?

Overnight parking is not permitted on festival grounds.

What vehicles are not permitted?

Campers, large vans, and buses are not permitted in the regular visitor parking area. These vehicles must be parked at designated camper or bus parking facilities elsewhere.

Is parking within walking distance of the castle?

For Vorden: Yes. The official parking areas are within reasonable walking distance of the event entrance. Please note that the route may include grass paths or uneven terrain.

For Arcen: There are two official parking areas:

Near Castle: Located within reasonable walking distance of the castle.

Lomm Parking: Located approximately 3 km from the castle. A free shuttle bus is provided to transport visitors between the parking area and the castle. The shuttle bus from Parking Lomm will depart from the bus stop near the office of the Limburgs Landscape Foundation (Rijksstraatweg 1, Lomm).

We recommend wearing comfortable footwear, as some walking may be required.

Elfia Ball – Mirror, Mirror on the Ball

What is Mirror, Mirror on the Ball?

At Elfia Arcen 2026, step into a magical ballroom experience where Elfians can dance, celebrate, and immerse themselves in the fantasy atmosphere of the Kingdom, inspired by this year's fairytale theme: Mirror, Mirror on the Ball.

How do I gain entry?

Entry is free, but a ballroom wristband is required. Wristbands become available at 17:00 at the Tourist Information Booth. Guests must register in person, present a valid ID, and be 18 years of age or older.

How many guests can attend?

Is there a Passport stamp?

Yes. Elfia Passport holders can collect an exclusive Ballroom Stamp.

Elfian Temptation

What is Elfia Temptation?

An exclusive 18+ fantasy show at Elfia Arcen 2026. Expect an experience that is playful and daring, but never vulgar.

How do I join?

Registration opens at 15:00 at the Tourist Information Booth. Entry is free for Saturday visitors, but only guests aged 18 and above may participate. Registration must be completed in person and a valid ID is required.

How many guests can attend?

Only 500 guests can participate. Once all places have been filled, registration will close.

Is there a Passport stamp?

Yes. Every accepted guest receives a special admission wristband and can collect a unique Elfia Temptation stamp for their Elfia Passport.
