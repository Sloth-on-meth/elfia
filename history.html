Elfia Arcen 2024

21-22 September 2024

Special Theme Zones - Giostra Fortunia

Giostra Fortunia continued to be one of the most anticipated places in the kingdom. This year, The Masquerade Ball lit up the night as guests danced beneath the stars while acrobats and fire artists turned the stage into a dreamlike spectacle. Acrobatic Dreams amazed with their soaring routines, Elements of Fire ignited the crowd with a pirate-inspired fire show, and Perry guided the evening with flair and humor. The return of Fabius the Jester kept the audience laughing, while the young magician Super Ale showed that Elfia'`s magic is also carried forward by new generations.

Music and Performances

As usual, music filled the gardens across both days. Saturday featured the high-energy folk punk of The O'Reillys and the Paddyhats alongside Celtic Giants, Pyrolysis, and Eihwar, ensuring the stages were alive from morning to night. Sunday followed with the legendary Celtic rock of The Dolmen, the worldly sounds of The Trouble Notes, and groups like Irdorath, Waldkauz, and Thundercrow, drawing visitors together for a finale that captured Elfia's spirit of diversity and celebration.

Workshops

The creative side of Elfia thrived through its workshops and competitions. Longtime festival contributors such as Aylin Lab returned with a hands-on session in costume design, while Yuji Koi inspired cosplayers with her renowned cosplay makeup masterclass. Newer perspectives came through Geheichou and Ayuru's social media workshop, which reflected how Elfia continues to evolve alongside its creative community.

Costume Parade and Cosplays

The Costume Parade and Cosplay Competition remained one of the highlights of the weekend. Hundreds of participants brought months of dedication to life, stepping onto the stage in front of cheering crowds to display craftsmanship, performance, and imagination. This year's judging panel featured a line-up of internationally respected names from the cosplay world. Aylin Lab, admired for her intricate Wonder Woman designs, lent her eye for precision and bold creativity. Yuji Koi, whose work has set standards across the cosplay community, brought her expertise in makeup and costume design. Meanwhile, Ayuru added her knowledge of makeup artistry and costume creation, with a sharp attention to detail and character portrayal. Finally, Geheichou, with experience as an official cosplayer for major gaming companies brought his charisma and understanding of how costumes come alive through performance. Their different perspectives shaped how the winners were chosen and kept the competition dynamic and engaging to watch.

Exhibitors and Marketplaces

Around the castle grounds, the marketplaces carried on the tradition of discovery that Elfia is known for. The Bizarre Bazaar, the Fantasy Faire, the Medieval Meadow, and the Witchery Market stretched across the gardens with stalls overflowing with handmade treasures. Visitors could browse everything from armor, gowns, and jewelry to books, leatherwork, ceramics, and fantastical props.

Food and Feasting

Food and drink stalls also added to the atmosphere, offering hearty meals, sweet treats, and drinks that kept travelers refreshed throughout the day. These blend of exhibitors, food stalls, and themed corners indeed made the grounds feel endlessly alive, with each step offering a new discovery.

Elfia Arcen 2023

16-17 September 2023

Costume Parade and Cosplays

The beloved Costume Parade once again stood as the crown jewel of Elfia Arcen. Participants filled the paths in dazzling attire, stepping into roles drawn from myth, legend, literature, and film. From the commanding presence of wizards like Dumbledore and Gandalf, to the elegance of Disney royals such as Princess Merida and her mother, to the fierce warriors and dreamers of How to Train Your Dragon, to the iconic characters like Maleficent, each costume was a masterpiece of imagination. With every detail, from hand-stitched armor to carefully styled wigs, highly dedicated Elfians reflected artistry, dedication, and a love for storytelling. In that spirit, the parade became a true celebration of the creativity and passion that unite Elfians year after year.

Music and Performances

Audiences were treated to a program that blended tradition with wonder at every turn. On Sunday, the Ukrainian Liberation Orchestra filled the air with classical music inspired by beloved fairy tales, from Mozart's Die Zauberflöte to the timeless melodies of Grieg's Peer Gynt. Their performance gave the gardens a magical soundtrack, as if the castle itself had come alive with music. Folk energy took over with Harmony Glen, whose lively tunes drew crowds together and set a joyful rhythm across the grounds. Those who wanted to do more than listen could join in the Balfolk workshop, learning the steps and feeling the shared heartbeat of dance.

Elk najaar in de tuinen

Elk najaar ontwaken de Kasteeltuinen Arcen wanneer Elfia het terrein vult met fantasie en creativiteit. Kostuums en muziek slingeren zich langs vijvers, bruggen en bloeiende paden. Met de Elfianen als hart wordt het historische terrein een plek waar verbeelding tot bloei komt.
