Icons on the map mark first aid, toilets, catering, lockers, changing rooms and the Elfia Elixir Bar.

Finding what you need

First aid & toilets

At several points across the grounds — look for the cross or toilet icon on the map. Some are also marked wheelchair-accessible.

Food & drink

Catering points (tankard icon) are spread across the themed areas; the Elfia Elixir Bar (potion icon) sits at a few fixed spots.

Lockers & changing rooms

Lockers by the keyhole icon, changing rooms by the purple icon — both at several points on the grounds.

Accessibility

Wheelchair-accessible toilets are marked with a separate icon on the map. See also accessible parking.
