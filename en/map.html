Finding what you need

First aid

At the Entrance (1).

Toilets

Entrance (1), Castle Arcen (2), Coach house (3), Bergpaviljoen (16), Plaza Verde (17), Knight’s Tents (36).

Food & drink

Coach house (3), Bergpaviljoen (16), Plaza Verde Bar (18, limited hours), Knight’s Tents (36, limited hours), kiosks (24, 40).

Accessibility

Wheelchair hire at the Entrance (1); a lift is available at Castle Arcen (2). The rock garden and miniature golf (15) is not wheelchair accessible. See accessible parking.
