What Awaits You

Ten themed realms, live music, parades and guests across two days in the castle gardens.

The ten realms

Fable Forge

Step into Fable Forge, Elfia’s hub of creativity. Join workshops on costumes, art, performance, and crafts, or simply watch the makers at work.

Area 51

In Area 51, shopping goes light years beyond ordinary with exclusive items, cosmic curiosities, and stellar souvenirs.

The Machinarium

In our Steampunk Zone, the hiss of steam and grind of engines set the stage for rare artifacts, handcrafted gear, and mechanical wonders.

Giostra Fortunia

Enter Giostra Fortunia, a dazzling spiegeltent with parades, acrobatics, burlesque, and magic — sometimes even the unexpected.

Bard’s Theater

Step into Bard’s Theater, where powerful performances draw you into the story. Here, art, music, and history meet on one stage.

The Lost World

Guided by brave rangers, explore wild trails, hear ancient roars, and come face-to-face with dinosaurs.

Black Skull Bay

A pirate haven of hidden treasures and daring adventures, where rogues and sailors test their courage.

Music Court

At Elfia’s heart, a vibrant stage brings crowds together to dance, hold hands, and celebrate as live bands fill the air.

Folk Nexus

A space where cultures meet through the joy of dance. Traditional styles like Balfolk and Hungarian dance come together.

Odin’s Vale

Step into the world of the Vikings, where Odin’s wisdom guides you through timeless sagas amid roaring drums.

Highlights

Acts, guests and traditions featured at Elfia Arcen.

New Elfia Trophy

A new tradition is born! Costume Parade winners now earn the Elfia Trophy, with a new gem colour each year.

Sunfire Band

A Western folk sensation blending Americana, country, and rock with fiery Old West spirit.

The Groggy Dogs

The Groggy Dogs bring rowdy pirate energy — punk, metal, ska, and sea shanties fired by Celtic spirit.

Cosplayshop.be

Cosplayshop.be supports Elfia’s Costume Parades, helping creators craft magic with top-quality materials.

Anhyra

Award-winning Spanish cosplayer Anhyra joined Elfia as guest and judge.

Don Esteban

Austria’s Eurocosplay Champion Don Esteban brought his award-winning skill to Elfia as guest.

The Trouble Notes

The Trouble Notes light up Elfia with folk-rock, improv, and jam sessions that blend musical worlds.

Mermaids and Mermen

Elfia’s waters come alive with real mermaids and mermen, turning fantasy into shimmering reality.

Envy Peru

Drag Race Holland’s first winner Envy Peru lit up Elfia with her style and inspiration.

ChelseaBoy

ChelseaBoy captivated audiences at Elfia Arcen 2025 with futuristic drag and avant-garde looks.

Workshops

Elfia inspires creativity through workshops in costume creation, burlesque, taxidermy, and more.
