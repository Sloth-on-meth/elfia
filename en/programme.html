What Awaits You

13 themed areas, live music, parades and guests across two days in the castle gardens.

Where does this come from? The descriptions are taken straight from Elfia’s programme API, unedited. That API’s area list does not match the map — it holds superseded names and misses areas — so the map is the spine here and the leftovers are listed separately. This is not an official Elfia publication.

The areas

The 13 numbered themed areas on the official map, in the order they are numbered.

Keep one hand on your coin pouch... and the other on your sense of adventure. Black Skull Bay is a bustling pirate haven filled with daring rogues, lively entertainment, and enough sea shanties to have every sailor singing along.

Dance beneath the open sky and sing along with the Kingdom. The Music Court is where unforgettable performances, festival anthems, and magical moments come together. Gather with fellow Elfians and let the music carry you from the first note to the final encore.

The Garden of Eve

Folk Faire

The Zen Garden

Let every step bring a little more balance. A tranquil garden where graceful landscapes and peaceful moments invite quiet reflection.

Bandits Creek

The Copper Quarter · 19 stalls

Leave room in your satchel... you'll need it. Every corner is filled with handcrafted marvels, extraordinary makers, and hidden treasures waiting to find their next keeper.

Viking Village · 26 stalls

A true wanderer never follows just one path...Meander through a lively village where remarkable makers, extraordinary creations, and unexpected discoveries await around every corner

Willow Way · 15 stalls

Follow the dancing leaves... they'll lead you somewhere wonderful. Beneath the willow branches, talented makers, handcrafted wonders, and a little woodland magic await.

Avalon · 11 stalls

If the mist begins to roll in... you're exactly where you should be. Wander the tranquil lakeside, where enchanting discoveries and whispers of Avalon linger in the air.

Merlin's Sanctum · 3 stalls

Mind the spellbooks... they have a habit of choosing their readers. Ancient magic lingers in every corner, where remarkable discoveries and timeless mysteries await.

Plan ahead

Saturday

  • Costume Parades — be seen, be remembered.
  • Mirror, Mirror on the Ball — an enchanting fantasy ball of dancing, music and storybook magic.
  • Elfia Temptation — an 18+ experience.

Sunday

  • Costume Catwalk — no judges, no prizes, no winners; just a stage full of extraordinary characters.
  • Fandom Embassies — enter your fandom.
  • Creatures at the waterfalls… and where you least expect them.

Registration for Elfia Temptation opens at 14:30; wristbands for the ball are available from 17:00. Both at the information booth (Elfia Treasures, by Music Court), both free, in person only and with valid ID showing you are 18+.
