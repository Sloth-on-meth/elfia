Frequently Asked Questions

The short answers. The full detail lives in the visitor policy.

When is Elfia Arcen 2026?

Saturday 19 and Sunday 20 September 2026. Saturday 10:00–22:00, Sunday 10:00–19:00.

Where is it?

Castle Gardens Arcen, Lingsforterweg 26, 5944 BE Arcen, Netherlands.

Can I leave and come back?

Yes. For 2026 re-entry is unlimited — leave and return as many times as you like, via the official exit points.

What about children?

Children aged 0 to 2 enter free. Little Elf tickets cover ages 3–11.

Can I bring my dog?

Yes, on a leash. Horses, birds and ferrets (leashed) are welcome too. Reptiles, lizards, rabbits and other exotic pets are not. Dogs are not allowed inside Castle Arcen itself.

Can I bring my metal sword?

No. Since November 2025 no metal weapons of any kind are permitted, even decorative, completely blunt, or fixed to your costume. Only soft non-metal props are allowed.

Can I bring my own food and drink?

Generally not. Permitted: one sealed plastic water bottle up to 500 ml, baby food, and food or drink for medical or dietary needs (medical proof may be requested). Glass is prohibited everywhere.

Where can I smoke?

Castle Gardens Arcen is smoke-free. Smoking and vaping are only allowed in the designated smoking areas, marked on the map. Cannabis is prohibited everywhere.

Can I fly a drone?

No. Drones are prohibited for everyone, including photographers and media. Flying over events and crowds is forbidden by law.

Is the site wheelchair accessible?

Largely yes. Wheelchair hire is available at the entrance and there is a lift at Castle Arcen. The rock garden and miniature golf (15) is not wheelchair accessible. Dedicated parking is available with an officially recognised disabled parking permit.

Can I arrive by horse?

Yes, but only five riders per day can park a horse. A horse parking ticket costs €1 and includes hay and fresh water.

Will my bag be checked?

Possibly. Security carries out bag checks at entry, always politely and respectfully.

Note: rules can differ from edition to edition. These answers apply to Arcen; always check the visitor policy for the location you are visiting.
