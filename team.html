Het team

De kunstenaars en productie-elfen achter het Koninkrijk.

Onze kunstenaars

Darja Blinoua

Manga Artist

Darja Blinoua, an Estonian freelance illustrator and graphic designer, is the artist behind Drach-oc. Her major contributions were to the Elfia Herald and the Elfia x Drach-oc chocolate packaging (Elfia Arcen 2025). Darja joined the team as the manga artist, specializing in illustrating dragons. Her work emphasized movement, anatomical accuracy, and emotional depth. Her style now merges Eastern manga with Western graphic sensibility, defining Drach-oc's unique visual language.

Steven Stoffels

Music Composer

Steven began playing the saxophone at just eight years old, and his early talent led him into a lifelong journey of music. He went on to study Classical Composition at the Conservatory of Tilburg, followed by a bachelor's program in Film Music at the Amsterdam Conservatory, and later graduated cum laude with a Master of Music in Film Scoring from the renowned Berklee College of Music. Throughout his career, Steven has written music for films, commercials, and live orchestral recordings across Europe, as well as worked as an orchestrator. You may know his work from Magic Forest, Crossing Bunny Bridge, Schoenmaten, and the official KNVB Anthems for the Dutch national football association. Today, Steven collaborates with Elfia as the festival's music composer, creating its signature theme and the tracks that shape the festival's atmosphere. Across his compositions, you can hear the spirit of the Kingdom of Elfia, its stories, its characters, and the world that binds them together.

Desiree Reiling

Illustrator

Desiree Reiling, a freelance designer and illustrator, uses her passion for fantasy to create playful visual stories. With a background in Visual Communication, design, and marketing, she crafts illustrative backgrounds for Elfia's interactive website, enhancing the visual appeal of its worlds and locations.

Luso Yong

Game Developer

Luso Yong is the Founder and Creative Director of LusoLab, an indie game development and creative-tech studio based in Malaysia. As a game developer with a passion for interactive storytelling and world-building, Luso built LusoLab to bring imaginative digital experiences to life through games, animation, and web-based interactive design. Since its founding, LusoLab has worked on a variety of creative projects ranging from game art and character design to full game development and interactive platforms. This includes mobile and virtual projects in 2D and 3D game art, edutainment, and environment concepts. For Elfia and the world of Drach-oc, Luso has developed our new interactive website, a dynamic digital experience designed to immerse visitors in the lore, visuals, and storytelling of both Elfia and the Planet Eve. This project features mini-games, web comics, interactive environments, and narrative elements that bring our worlds alive online, giving fans a rich, playful, and engaging gateway into the realms we create.

Arina Stretškova

Tattoo Artist

Arina (@barbiesundaytattoos) is a full-time tattoo artist from Estonia, based in Tartu, specializing in traditional flora and animal motifs. She joined the team in October 2024. A former classmate and long-time friend of lead artist Darja from Pallas University of Applied Sciences, Arina contributes base coloring for Drach-oc and offers creative design solutions in visual discussions.

Aylin Lab

Costume Designer

Aylin Lab is a renowned artisan and cosplayer celebrated for high-quality, intricate costumes and props. Her work blends materials like foam, thermoplastics (Worbla), 3D printing, resin, and natural elements. Inspired by video games and film, Aylin's precision has earned international recognition, including representing Italy in major competitions. Within the Kingdom of Elfia, Aylin is a prominent judge and sponsor. She notably handcrafted Elfia's first exclusive Costume Parade Trophy, a unique, limited-edition piece requiring months of intricate work.

Anastasia Tšehhonadskaja

Graphic Designer

Anastasia, also known as astilte.tatts, is an artist from Estonia, currently based in Tallinn. She holds a fine-arts degree and has a passion for tattooing, creating dark and graphic pieces. Anastasia joined the team in February 2024 through creative acquaintances. She supports Drach-oc and Darja by preparing the base coloring for characters and environments, giving each page a consistent foundation before the final rendering.

Manu MFK Cosplay

Creative Designer

Manu MFK Cosplay is a professional Cosmaker, Artisan, Performer, Creative, and 3D Designer specializing in technical costume creation. Known for his dramatic, highly transformative "FREAK" characters, his artistic focus is complex make-up and prosthetic applications. In partnership with Manuel D'Andrea and Aylin Lab, he helped create the exclusive 2025 Elfia Trophy.

Manuel D'Andrea

Costume Designer

Manuel D'Andrea is an acclaimed costume designer, art director, and creative visionary known for transforming fantasy worlds into immersive, tangible experiences. With over 70 appearances as a Guest in international events and with a background rooted in cinematic craftsmanship, Manuel has designed and constructed intricate costumes for Hollywood production houses like 20th Century Fox and Disney, live entertainment and high-profile events earning recognition for his meticulous detailing, sculptural approach and ability to merge storytelling with fashion-driven aesthetics. With his artistic approach, he oversees world-building on a grand scale: from themed zones and atmospheric environments to character design and stage direction. His artistic signature blends theatrical flair with contemporary sophistication, crafting experiences that feel both magical and believable; this creative vision extends into Elfia's new merchandise line as well, which Manuel personally curates and designs.

Stefan Struik

Author

Stefan Struik, originally trained in law, is the creative force behind the Elfia fantasy festival and the dark-fantasy manga, Drach-oc. His career began writing detective series and TV dramas, skills he used to create Elfia, one of Western Europe's most iconic fantasy festivals, offering an escape into imagination. This passion for fictional worlds also led to Drach-oc, a manga where the dragon is the protagonist and narrator.

Over Elfia

Elfia is niet zomaar een festival — het is het eerste openlucht cosplay- en kostuumkoninkrijk ter wereld. Opgericht in 2001 als de Elf Fantasy Fair, koos het vanaf het begin een eigen weg: verbeelding verweven met erfgoed, en verhalen tot leven gebracht bij kastelen en historische locaties.

Bijna vijfentwintig jaar later blijft het groeien en zichzelf opnieuw uitvinden. Opgericht door Stefan Struik is het een steeds groter wordend universum, dat traditie eert en tegelijk voortdurend nieuwe ervaringen toevoegt.

Het is een koninkrijk, een gemeenschap en een doorlopend verhaal. En wie er eenmaal binnenstapt, laat het niet meer los.

Waar het begon

In april 2001 gingen de poorten van Archeon open voor de allereerste Elf Fantasy Fair. In de houten en stenen hal van het klooster spraken Brian Froud, John Howe, Raymond E. Feist, Terry Pratchett en Robert Jordan een betoverd publiek toe. De Romeinse wijk telde slechts twintig kraampjes met ambacht, boeken en wonderlijke vondsten.

Er stond een kostuumcatwalk gepland in het prehistorische dorp, maar die moest worden afgelast toen een klein kind verkleed als ork zoek raakte — het dook later op bij de ingang en joeg de middeleeuwse wachters daar de stuipen op het lijf.
